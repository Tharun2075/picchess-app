        About PicChess

        Recognition runs entirely in your browser: the picture never leaves your device, and there is no server or account.

        • Piece model: PicChess squares-screen (our own, trained on rendered boards in 30 piece sets and many themes).
        • Runtime: onnxruntime-web (MIT, Microsoft).
        • Board: cm-chessboard (MIT). Pieces: Chessnut (Apache-2.0). Rules: chess.js (BSD-2). Engine: Stockfish (GPL-3, licence).
        • Full list with licence files: Licences.

        Accuracy counter: this device keeps a count of how many squares you corrected after each scan. It is stored only in this browser and never sent anywhere.
